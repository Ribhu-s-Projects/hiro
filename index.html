Questions

Why does applying early matter?
A recruiter can start reviewing and interviewing before a posting closes. Finding a relevant opening while it's fresh gives you a chance to be considered earlier in that process. It doesn't guarantee a reply or an interview.
Does an ATS automatically reject every resume without exact keywords?
No. An applicant tracking system stores and organizes applications. Recruiters can use skill searches, filters and screening answers; some employers also use automated matching. The setup varies. Clear, truthful evidence of the role's requirements helps both the search and the human review.
How does Hiro get to know me?
Through a voice conversation about your career: what you worked on, the challenges you faced and what changed. Hiro uses those details, alongside the resumes and profiles you share, to understand the experience behind your bullet points.
Which companies do you watch?
Funded startups, product companies and global teams with India offices. Name a company and Hiro adds it.
Do you apply for me?
No. You decide and you apply. An “apply for me” option comes later, and only for roles you approve.
What happens to my resume and voice note?
Hiro uses them only to match you. Ask in the chat and they're deleted.
I'm not okay right now. Can I talk to someone?
Yes. Pick “Someone to talk to” and one of us replies within a day. For urgent support, Tele-MANAS on 14416 is free and open all day, every day.
